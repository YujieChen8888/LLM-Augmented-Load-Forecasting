Method

Structure the event, preserve its scope, and fuse it with the load context.

  1. 01

    Structure the news

    Separate reported facts from anticipated conditions and retain load-relevant events.

  2. 02

Discussion

01

When does news improve load forecasting?

News is useful when it is relevant to the target load and adds information beyond the available numerical inputs.

Information relevance

Load-relevant events are sparse in noisy news. Filtering and structuring them improves MAE; raw news alone performs slightly worse than excluding news.

Incremental information

Shock-period MAE reduction from adding news:

Historical weather reduces the extra information news provides, while some complementary value remains during shocks.

In other periods with historical weather, news increases MAE by 1.14%: 178.54 to 180.57 MW.

Three-seed means · Lower is better

  1. 01

    Pretraining provides a useful semantic prior.

    With the same architecture and tokenizer, pretrained MiniLM reduces MAE by 2.75% and RMSE by 2.98% versus random initialization. Both frozen encoders are compared under matched settings; both metrics improve across all three seeds.

  2. 02

    Larger encoders do not guarantee better forecasts.

    The 8B encoder improves on the 1B model, but compact MiniLM achieves the lowest errors among the tested encoders.

Encoder architectures and representation dimensions also differ; these comparisons do not isolate model size alone.

The takeaway

Structured events, fused with load context, improve forecasting under external shocks.
